The story

The right hand ended. The left hand began.

Sayive's original competitive weapon was his right hand. At its prime, he describes it as strong enough to compete against athletes in the 70 kg and 80 kg categories.

In October 2025, during a tournament, his right arm was injured around the elbow. Although the arm later recovered for normal use, it never returned to its former level for armwrestling. Instead of quitting, Sayive made a difficult decision: build a new weapon.

He switched his focus to the left hand. At the beginning, the left was extremely weak — even lifting 10 kg was difficult. After training consistently for nearly a month, he began feeling noticeable progress in lifting and arm-specific training.

The left hand is still developing. The journey is intentionally being documented from the beginning.

The style

Drag. Hook. Finish.

“Draghooker” reflects the direction Sayive is building toward: a powerful inside game combining hand control, dragging pressure, a natural hook, and an explosive finish. The current style is still developing rather than being presented as a finished professional system.

The mission

Not a title yet. A target.

“I want to become the strongest left-hand arm wrestler in Nagaland.”

That is the immediate ambition. The goal is not being claimed as an achievement today; it is the reason for the work.

The long-term dream goes beyond Nagaland: become a 55 kg left-hand champion, become known internationally, develop strong sportsmanship, move through higher weight divisions over time, and one day chase the dream of becoming one of the world's greatest arm wrestlers.

Alizhan Muratov is the main inspiration — especially his explosive strength, technique, training and dominant left-hand performances.

“The right hand was my past.
The left hand is my mission.”
